Official Data Quality, Completeness & Integrity Audit

Programmatic validation findings across all 1,981 central sector projects in the official datasets.

[DATA QUALITY REPORT]

1. Sentinel Value: Revised Cost = 0

905 Projects (45.7%)

In the raw CSV, a Revised Cost of 0 indicates that the project budget has not yet undergone revision. Treating this naively as numeric zero would falsely compute a -100% cost reduction. Our engine flags this with NOT_YET_REVISED_COST.

2. Missing Revised End Dates

354 Projects (17.9%)

17.9% of project rows record no revised completion date. These represent newly sanctioned works or reporting latency. They are quarantined from schedule delay regression to prevent training label corruption. Tagged with MISSING_REVISED_DATE.

3. Expenditure Exceeding Original Sanction

160 Projects (8.1%)

Cumulative disbursements have exceeded 100% of sanctioned outlay ahead of commissioning. This represents active capital exhaustion risk where revised approvals are lagging behind on-ground spend. Tagged with EXPENDITURE_EXCEEDS_ORIGINAL.

4. Schedule Outlier Clipping & Dates

Dates up to 2056

Projects like "Badam Coal Mining project of NTPC" have original end dates extending to 2056. Calculated delays range from -10,867 to +9,131 days (~25 years). Our model winsorizes schedule targets between [-365, 3650] days to prevent metric distortion.

5. Anomaly & Extreme Outlier Isolation

1,189 Projects Audited

Automated rules isolate extreme anomalies (cost escalation >300%, milestone target dates beyond 2035, or zero-cost revisions) with explicit audit flags, ensuring transparent governance reporting without corrupting predictive models.
